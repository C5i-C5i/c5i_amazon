HEURISTIC EVALUATION

Our approach: Evidence-based research and insight mining.

A repeatable expert-review framework to find, score and operationalize UX improvements.

Frameworks We Apply

  • Nielsen's 10 usability heuristics
  • WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility checks
  • Shneiderman's 8 golden rules
  • Gestalt design principles
  • ISO 9241-110 dialogue principles
  • Platform guidelines (iOS HIG, Material)
  • Cognitive walkthrough
  • Conversational and AI UX heuristics
  • Custom heuristics for your domain

Methodology

1 Scope and set selection

Screens and flows in scope, heuristic set (Nielsen / WCAG / custom), severity scale (0-4) agreed with stakeholders.

2 Expert evaluation

Evaluators independently review the interface against the set and log each violation with location and severity.

3 Aggregation and scoring

Findings merged and de-duplicated; severity scored on frequency, impact and persistence.

4 Reporting

Issue log with screenshots, heuristic violated, severity, and fix recommendation; summary for stakeholders.

5 Debrief and handoff

Walkthrough with design and PM, severity disagreements resolved, issues moved into the backlog.

Outputs Provided

  • Severity-scored issue log (0-4)
  • Annotated screenshots of each issue
  • Heuristic scorecard and heatmap
  • Prioritized fix recommendations
  • Quick wins vs. long-term fixes
  • Competitor benchmark comparison
  • Executive readout and next steps
